Honest comparison

Pragnosia vs. today's LLMs

A spinning brain and a GPT-style model are built on opposite bets. Here is exactly where each one wins and where each one loses. No spin (well… some spin).

The fundamental difference

Two opposite bets about thinking

Today's LLMs

Predict the next word, at huge scale

  • Settle on a representation in one forward pass attention looks up patterns.
  • Frozen after training. Learning new facts means fine-tuning or stuffing them into the prompt.
  • Knowledge lives in billions of weights vast, but fixed and hard to inspect.
  • Confidence is bolted on they often state wrong things fluently.
  • Power from scale: trillions of tokens, billions–trillions of parameters.
Pragnosia

Spin as the core mixer, and keep living

  • Spins a transformer/SSM hybrid (à la Mamba/RWKV) where a diagonal-complex rotational recurrence (a parallel associative scan) is the core token-mixer, carrying state in its phase.
  • Continual. Learns new facts online and remembers them across sessions no retraining.
  • Knowledge can be taught and recalled on the fly, with surprise-modulated plasticity.
  • It abstains on the clearly-unknowable it abstains (“I don't know”) from its own uncertainty — though at 284M this signal is scale-limited, not solved.
  • Parameter-efficient when spin is the core: a param-matched test shows the spin-dominant design beats an attention-only baseline by ~21%, and at 284M the spin carrier is provably the load-bearing core (ablate it → perplexity 306× worse vs 3.4× for attention) single-seed and not yet at 1B, but a properly-controlled at-scale signal.

The honest ledger

Pros & cons of the spinning approach

Where Pragnosia wins

  • Continual learning learns and persists new facts across sessions without full retraining; general skills are retained (specific-fact editing still has a stability–plasticity tension).
  • Abstains, honestly says “I don't know” on the clearly-unknowable; it does not yet catch confident-but-wrong facts (honesty is an open problem at this scale).
  • A rigorous causal probe we measure whether the carrier is actually used: a carried-state transplant on single-carrier models (which caught the 1.4B side-channel drift below) and direct ablation on the spin-dominant design (which showed the 284M carrier is load-bearing, 306×).
  • Tiny & cheap runs and trains on a single laptop GPU; ~700 MB on disk.
  • Brain-like learning surprise-modulated plasticity: learns hard on the new, gently on the familiar.
  • Nothing hardcoded what matters, when to abstain, what to recall: all derived from data, re-tuned as it grows.

Where it loses (for now)

  • The scaled 1.4B was built backwards growth diluted the single spin carrier to 0.37% of the parameters, so it's effectively an attention transformer with a vestigial spin side-channel (causal signal just 0.028% of the loss). The fix making spin the core mixer wins ~21% at matched params and is now validated at 284M (carrier load-bearing: 306× ablation cost), but is still single-seed and not yet at 1B.
  • Weak multi-hop composition undertrained for its grown size; relational analogy, multi-step deduction and systematic generalization still lag. More tokens, not more parameters, is the lever.
  • Can be confidently wrong on familiar-but-incorrect facts (the same failure as big LLMs).
  • Continual learning has a stability–plasticity tension teaching a fact hard enough to recall it can interfere with close neighbours (teaching a made-up capital nudged a real country's capital); teaching gently avoids that but under-learns. General skills are retained (no broad forgetting), but reliable single-fact editing isn't solved yet.
  • Not a finished product it's a research substrate proving mechanisms, not a polished assistant.
  • Emergence is early, not broad at the new 284M spin-dominant scale, arithmetic (3/3), world-knowledge (2/3) and theory-of-mind (2/2) genuinely emerged (up from faint seeds at 27M), but multi-step, counterfactual and causal reasoning are still 0/2 the next axis. And the honesty gate is not solved: consistency-voting can't separate knowledge from confident confabulation at 284M (a known fact and an unknowable score the same) it needs scale or a redesigned signal.

The bottom line

An LLM is a brilliant, frozen library enormous knowledge, but it stops learning the day training ends and will fluently invent an answer rather than admit ignorance. Pragnosia is a tiny, living mind it knows far less, but it keeps learning across sessions, tries to abstain at the edge of its knowledge (imperfectly), and carries its computation in a spinning state we can causally probe. It's a transformer/SSM hybrid where spin is meant to be the core mixer and our honest, measured finding is that it's most parameter-efficient exactly when spin is the core, now shown at 284M where the spin carrier is provably load-bearing (a result still to be fully proven multi-seed at 1B). The bet: alive, honest, and causally-probeable are what a frozen trillion-parameter model fundamentally lacks. Scale makes a better library; this is an attempt at a better brain.
