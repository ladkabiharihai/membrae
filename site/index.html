The core idea

An answer you can spin

🌀 Imagine a tiny dancer that never stops twirling. Where it's pointing at the right moment is the answer. Most AIs are like a ball rolling to a stop in a bowl they settle. Pragnosia keeps spinning, and the answer lives in the direction it spins.

The precise version

The carrier is a linear-recurrent unit (LRU / S5-style): h_t = λ ⊙ h_{t−1} + b_t, with λ_j = exp(−exp(ν_j))·e^{iθ_j} each channel is a damped complex oscillator at its own frequency, and the answer is carried in the phase. b_t is a gated complex input; the readout is C·[Re h ; Im h], added back through a learned gate σ(γ). Because λ is diagonal, the recurrence is matmul-free O(T·d) and associative so it runs not as a sequential loop but as a log-depth parallel associative scan. This is the only recurrence in the code, which makes Pragnosia a transformer/SSM hybrid in the family of Mamba / RWKV but where spin is meant to be the core token-mixer. The honest twist: the first scaled model accidentally made spin a tiny side-channel; built correctly (spin-dominant), a param-matched test shows it wins by ~21% and at 284M the carrier becomes load-bearing (see the journey below).

How we know it's real

The ablation test

🧪 If the spinning carrier really does the thinking, then switching it off should break the model. At our 284M spin-dominant scale we tried exactly that: zero out the spin carrier and perplexity explodes ~306×. Zero out attention instead and it barely moves (~3.4×). The carrier isn't decoration it's the load-bearing core.

The measured numbers

On the 284M spin-dominant model (val ppl ≈ 25), zeroing the spin carrier drives perplexity to 7716 (a 306× collapse); zeroing attention only takes it to 85 (3.4×). In the spin-dominant build the carrier lives inside each block, so there is no separable carried state to splice ablation, not a state-swap, is the causal measure here. This is the exact inverse of the earlier 1.4B "wrong build," where a single bolted-on carrier contributed just 0.028% of the loss vestigial.

Inside the head

Spin as the core mixer, attention as a helper

Pragnosia is a transformer/SSM hybrid (think Mamba / RWKV). One config flag carrier ∈ {none, single, per_block, spin_dominant} wires the whole stack from pragnosia.json through training and growth. The bet, validated in spin_dominant, is that the spinning recurrence should be the core token-mixer, with attention a periodic helper.

Substrate

🌀 Spinning core

A diagonal-complex linear recurrence each channel a damped oscillator at its own frequency, the answer carried in phase. Diagonal λ makes it matmul-free O(T·d) and associative, so it runs as a parallel associative scan. torch.compile fuses the scan for ~2× throughput (49K → ~100K tok/s, bs 24, RTX 4060).

Mix

🔭 Spin-dominant layout

The validated design: a SpinBlock (carrier as mixer, strong gate) in 3 of every 4 layers, and a standard attention block every 4th. Attention gives fluent words; the carrier does the cross-token mixing in between. (The first 1.4B build accidentally did the inverse see the journey.)

Glue

🧭 Self-calibration

What words matter, when to abstain, the learning rate, when to grow all derived from data (self-information, equal-error-rate, Smith range-test, data budget), never hand-set, and re-derived as the brain grows.

THE WRONG BUILD

Scaled the wrong way first: a single bolted-on carrier

The first scaling put a single spin carrier after a transformer stack. As the model grew, its parameter share collapsed 5.76% (21M) → 1.90% (276M) → 0.37% (1.4B). At 1.4B (best val ppl 17.35) a carried-state intervention showed the carrier contributes just 0.028% of the loss vestigial. It also over-grew (an early trigger fired on any plateau, not true saturation). This is the "wrong build" reference, not the path.

THE FIX

Growth that governs itself

Growth now fires only on probe-confirmed saturation it briefly trains depth- and width-grown copies and keeps growth only if val loss beats the noise. No hardcoded size cap: the ceiling is the data budget (≈20 tokens/param) and VRAM. A gentle absolute-LR reset and an OOM-safe probe make it robust. Seen in production: a spin-dominant run grew itself 24M → 27.3M (4 → 5 layers), then stopped on its own at the data budget.

THE ABLATION

Param-matched: spin wins when it's the core mixer

A clean controlled test at ~37–46M (same tokens, same budget, single seed): spin-dominant 219 ppl vs attention-only 279 a ~21% win at matched parameters, also beating a per-block hybrid (228) and a param-matched transformer (270). Spin earns its weights precisely when it is the core token-mixer.

AT SCALE · the headline

284M, built spin-dominant the carrier IS the core

The first at-scale run of the intended design: a 284M brain (d=768, 20 layers, mlp_mult 9, spin as the token-mixer in 3 of every 4 blocks, attention every 4th) trained to step 418K on a 176.6B-token chain-of-thought corpus, best val ppl 24.54. The decisive causal test here is ablation (the carrier lives inside each block, so there's no separable state to swap): zero the spin carrier and perplexity explodes 25 → 7716 (306×); zero attention and it only goes 25 → 85 (3.4×). The carrier carries almost the entire computation the exact inverse of the vestigial 1.4B, and at 5× fewer parameters. Honest caveat: single-seed; 1B-scale + multi-seed, and a matched carrier="none" baseline, still to come.

AT SCALE · capability

World-knowledge, arithmetic and theory-of-mind emerged

On a greedy capability battery (27M laptop model in parens): arithmetic 3/3 (0/3), knowledge 2/3 (0/3), logic 2/2, theory-of-mind 2/2 (1/2), code 1/2, in-context binding 1/2. Samples: "capital of France → the city of Paris", "2+2 → 4", fluent stories. The weak axis is still multi-step 0/2, counterfactual 0/2, causal 0/2 the next target. Undertrained for size; more tokens is the lever.

No hype

What's honestly still hard

Every claim here has a number behind it including the limits.

  • The big model was built backwards but the fix now holds at scale. In the scaled 1.4B the spinning carrier is a single fixed layer that growth diluted to 0.37% of the parameters an attention-dominant transformer with a vestigial spin side-channel (causal signal just 0.028% of the loss). The intended design is spin as the core mixer; a param-matched test shows that wins by ~21% (219 vs 279 ppl), and the new 284M spin-dominant model proves the carrier is load-bearing at scale (ablate it → ppl 306× worse vs 3.4× for attention), and a 565M continuation now reaches val ppl ~22. The placement win is now multi-seed (3 seeds: attention 361 / spin 116 / real-no-phase 102 ± ~2), and a different recurrence as the core also wins, so it's about placement, not the complex phase. 1B replication is the remaining open item.
  • Undertrained for its size. Growth added capacity (1.4B) faster than the 3-day window could train it (Chinchilla wants ≈28B tokens for 1.4B; we fed far less). Perplexity is its best ever (17.35), but precise long-tail facts still wobble (e.g. "how many continents") more tokens, not more parameters, is the lever now.
  • Multi-hop composition is weak. Single-step facts, arithmetic and recall are real, but relational analogy (hand:glove::foot:?), compositional/systematic generalization, and multi-step deduction are still ~0 the undertrained-for-size signature, and a real gap. The fix is more training tokens, not prompting.
  • Identity isn't in the weights. Training "I am Pragnosia" directly bleeds ("capital of France → Pragnosia"), so identity is installed at runtime by the brain, not baked in. Self-knowledge as cognition, not a memorized string.
  • It's compute-bound, not frontier. Spin-dominant is actually slower than attention at short context (ctx-256): the scan does more work and can't be made in-place without breaking autograd. Its wins are recurrent inference (O(1)/token, no KV cache) and structurally long context (O(T) vs O(T²)). But the long-context advantage isn't free: a model trained on fresh 256-token windows doesn't yet use a cross-window carried state, so realizing it needs training with that carry (a continual long-context run is underway) today's effective context is still the trained window. torch.compile fuses the scan for ~2× training throughput (measured 49K → ~100K tok/s at bs=24 on a consumer RTX 4060). Honest emergence scores still sit ~30–45% and fluctuate strength comes from scale + data.
